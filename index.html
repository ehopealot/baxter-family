What it does

Just text it. Bax takes it from there.

Nothing to install and no app to open. You text a number, and Baxter works out what you need.

Keeps the week straight

Who's where, when, and what collides with what. Ask what Thursday looks like and get an answer, not a calendar to squint at.

Holds the list

Groceries, the hardware store, the things you keep meaning to deal with. Anyone in the house can add to it from wherever they are.

Nudges you

Ask for a reminder and it books it — once on Thursday, or every weekday at nine. It shows up whether or not anyone's around.

Shops around

Four dishwashers, three insurance quotes, the same flight on six sites. It reads the listings and tells you which one wins, and why.

Does the reading

The forty-page report, the school handbook, the terms nobody reads. You get the answer, not a stack of links.

Remembers

What you decided, what you tried, what everyone's allergic to. Nothing gets re-explained.

What we send
Replies to what you text Baxter, plus the reminders and alerts you set up yourself. No marketing, ever.
How often
Message frequency varies — Baxter answers when you text it and sends alerts when something you asked it to watch happens. There's no fixed schedule and no broadcast.

Stopping
Reply STOP at any time to end all messages, START to resume, or HELP for help. You can also email privacy@bax.bot.
