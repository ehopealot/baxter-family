What it does

Ask it like you'd ask a person.

Nothing to install and no app to open. Text it or email it, and Baxter works out what you need.

Remembers

What you decided, what you tried, what everyone's allergic to. Nothing gets re-explained, and nobody has to be the one who remembers.

Holds the list

Groceries, the hardware store, the thing you keep meaning to deal with. Add to it from wherever you are, and whoever gets to the shop first has the same list.

Keeps the week straight

Who's where, when, and what collides with what. Ask what Thursday looks like and get an answer, not a calendar to squint at.

Sorts out dinner

Tell it what's in the fridge and it comes back with something you can actually make tonight. Anything you're missing goes straight on the list.

Text it or email it

Your household gets its own address — smithfam@assistant.bax.bot — alongside a number you can text. Same Baxter either way, same memory, whichever one's nearer to hand.
